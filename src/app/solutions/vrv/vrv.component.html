VRV / VRF Systems


A VRV (Variable Refrigerant Volume) or VRF (Variable Refrigerant Flow) system is an advanced air conditioning technology that regulates the amount of refrigerant supplied to multiple indoor units based on real-time cooling or heating demand.

Unlike conventional HVAC systems that operate at fixed capacity, VRV and VRF systems continuously adjust compressor speed and refrigerant flow. This intelligent operation ensures that each zone receives the exact amount of cooling required, resulting in lower electricity consumption, greater comfort, and improved system performance.
