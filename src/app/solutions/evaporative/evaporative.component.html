Evaporative Cooling

Maintaining a comfortable and productive working environment in large industrial facilities can be challenging, especially where conventional air conditioning systems are expensive to install and operate. At Siddhivinayak Engineering Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (SVESPL), we provide high-performance Evaporative Cooling Systems designed to deliver fresh, cool air while significantly reducing energy consumption.

Our evaporative cooling solutions are engineered for factories, warehouses, production units, workshops, logistics centres, and other large-volume spaces where continuous ventilation and thermal comfort are essential. Using the natural process of water evaporation, these systems reduce indoor temperatures while improving air circulation, creating a healthier and more comfortable working environment.

With over 18 years of HVAC and MEP engineering experience, we understand that every industrial facility has unique operational requirements. Our engineering team carefully evaluates building dimensions, heat load, ventilation requirements, occupancy levels, and production processes before designing a customized evaporative cooling solution that maximizes efficiency and long-term performance.

Rather than offering standard products, we develop customized evaporative cooling systems based on airflow calculations, equipment sizing, duct layouts, and operational requirements. This engineering-first approach ensures optimum cooling performance and energy efficiency.



Quality Equipment Selection

The performance of an evaporative cooling system depends on the quality of its components. We select reliable cooling units, fans, pumps, ducts, and control systems from trusted manufacturers to ensure dependable operation and long service life.



Professional Installation

Our experienced installation teams follow structured engineering procedures to ensure every system is installed accurately, safely, and according to project specifications. Proper installation helps maximize cooling efficiency while minimizing maintenance requirements.



Testing & Commissioning
