HVAC Retrofit Solutions



Improve the performance of your existing HVAC system with a retrofit approach planned around your building, equipment and operational needs. An HVAC retrofit can help address ageing equipment, inconsistent comfort, recurring maintenance concerns and opportunities to use energy more efficiently-without automatically requiring a complete system replacement.

Replacing an entire heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC) system is not always the first or only option when performance starts to decline. In many facilities, a carefully assessed retrofit can improve selected parts of the system while making better use of existing infrastructure.

HVAC Retrofit Services and Upgrade Options

Ventilation requirements are easy to overlook during construction or renovation. However, an unsuitable arrangement can contribute to stuffy indoor spaces, uneven airflow, excess moisture, and uncomfortable working conditions.
A properly planned building ventilation system can help address these challenges.


HVAC System Assessment

Review the existing installation, reported issues and operating requirements. The assessment may include equipment condition, controls, air distribution, ventilation and maintenance history, depending on the agreed scope.



Controls and Automation Upgrades

Where compatible and technically suitable, control upgrades can help improve scheduling, temperature management and coordination between system components.



Air Distribution and Ventilation Improvements

Review airflow, fresh-air provision and ventilation performance to identify possible adjustments to ductwork, dampers, fans or related components.



Equipment Component Upgrades

Selected components may be repaired, refurbished or replaced when assessment shows that doing so is appropriate and compatible with the wider system.



Energy-Performance Improvements

Identify practical opportunities to reduce avoidable HVAC energy use, such as reviewing operating schedules, setpoints, controls and system performance. Actual savings depend on baseline conditions, usage and the implemented scope.



Phased HVAC Modernisation

For facilities that cannot stop operations for a full upgrade, a phased approach may be considered where technically feasible and safely planned.



Our HVAC Retrofit Approach

Understand the requirement:
Discuss the building type, operational priorities, comfort concerns and recurring HVAC issues.

Assess the existing system:
Review available equipment details and carry out a site assessment where agreed.

Identify practical options:
Compare potential improvements against compatibility, condition, performance goals, disruption and budget.

Define the scope:
Document the proposed work, assumptions, exclusions and any dependencies before implementation.

Plan and execute:
Coordinate work with the facility team and building operations, following applicable safety and site procedures.

Test and review:
Where included in the scope, check system operation after the upgrade and communicate any further recommendations.

Benefits of a Well-Planned HVAC Retrofit


  • Targets specific performance issues in an existing installation.
  • Can help extend the useful life of suitable equipment.
  • May improve comfort, airflow or control, depending on the scope.
  • Can provide an alternative to immediate full-system replacement where appropriate.
  • Allows upgrades to be considered in phases when site conditions permit.
  • Creates an opportunity to review energy use and maintenance priorities.

Note: Benefits are dependent on the condition of the existing system, the selected measures, installation quality, operation and maintenance. No specific energy savings or payback period should be promised without supporting analysis.
