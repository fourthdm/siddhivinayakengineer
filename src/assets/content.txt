Case Study · 01
Emerson Innovation Centre — VRF System Modernisation
Upgrade of a critical innovation facility where HVAC reliability directly impacted operational continuity and engineering performance.

Key Challenges
Obsolete air-conditioning system causing repeated operational failures
Rising maintenance overhead and inefficiency across the facility
Performance instability impacting engineering activity and uptime
Existing system installed by a competitor — using Emerson's own compressors

Engineering Approach
Detailed technical audit of the existing infrastructure, load patterns and failure history
Designed a 1200 HP VRF platform optimised for load adaptability and energy efficiency
Scalable system architecture aligned with real operational demand of the innovation facility
Zone-level redundancy built in to protect critical R&D activity

Execution Strategy
Consultative engineering-led engagement, not a vendor-style installation
Technical presentation and stakeholder validation of the proposed system
Precision execution sequenced to minimise disruption to ongoing R&D operations

Results & Performance
Commissioned within the planned timeline with no operational interruption
Stabilised system performance under continuous usage conditions
Reduced dependency on frequent maintenance cycles
Improved efficiency aligned with the facility's actual load patterns

Strategic Impact
Secured 600 HP VRF project for the Emerson Energy Export Centre
Awarded the complete HVAC revamp of EIC Pune
Established a long-term engineering partnership with Emerson
